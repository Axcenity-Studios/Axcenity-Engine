# CO9 part 2: hand-back (RA3 with the fitting core, refinement wave 4)

Branch `co9-fit-2`, from `main` after wave 3 (#427). Task: make it possible for a real garment to pass the fit gate (plan section 1's CO9 row, 11.5 R7; asset-quality.md §2, §3, §4).

**In one paragraph.** The body has swing correctives now, the sports bra's straps sit on the shoulders, and the test tee has an ordinary crew neck. With the arms raised the tee went from failing on every one of the 54 gate bodies (up to 16 mm) to failing on two (5.1 mm). The motion and footwear checks are built, as helpers a content track calls on its own items, and they run on the shipped items. **The main goal is not met: the test tee still fails the gate in the walk on all 54 bodies (30 mm, from 31.4).** The cause is not the swing. It is the arm hanging against the side: the tee's underarm was made with the arm at 45 degrees and can't fold into the crease. The boxer briefs' gusset case is a little smaller (13.3 mm, from 14.6) and not fixed. **The stylized clothes are not refitted.** I measured them, recorded them and captured them; their underwear still shows skin through in large blotches. Every number the gate and the checks still find is recorded as an open defect, at or under its wave-3 record.

## What was built

1. **Swing correctives** (`Corrective_Shoulder_Forward_*` full at 40 degrees, `_Back_*` full at 50):
   - **How they are made.** By delta mush in the QA swing poses, as the other shoulder shapes are, on top of `Lowered` (its offsets are taken off).
   - **The rule.** It turns about the axis across the body, in the upper arm's parent's rest space. That axis is at right angles to the raise axis, so a swing doesn't change the lowered shape's weight.
   - **The walk's angles.** I measured them in the engine: the starter Walk swings the upper arm back to 49 degrees and forward to 31, the Run to 76 and 36. The arm hangs 12 to 18 degrees out.
2. **The ramp is eased** (smoothstep) in `anim`, `avatar::fit` and the QA scripts. A straight ramp started a shape at full speed as the arm swung through hanging (Back to Forward in every step), and cloth carrying a large copy of the shape jolted in that frame. 0, 0.5 and 1 stay where they were.
3. **The bra's straps** sit over the top of the shoulder:
   - feminine frame: middle 9.5 cm from the body's middle, inner edge 8.2 cm;
   - masculine frame: 9.8 to 13 cm.
   - The skin under them is weighted to the neck at most 0.2 (it was 0.9, up to 1.50 m on the neck).
4. **The test tee:**
   - an ordinary crew neck (the opening 15.8 cm across, 14 mm wider than the neck at each side);
   - the shoulders let down to 14 mm over the skin;
   - the collar's edge settled onto the skin;
   - the bra is hidden from every capture view, with nothing let off at the collar (it was 20 and 17 corners).
5. **The underwear's push-out** includes the 47 realistic cast and body benchmarks' mixes.
   - I tried three other things; each was worse, so none is in the commit:
     - repeated passes over all states: rest 13.35 mm, but the walk 18.04 mm, over the record;
     - a one-ring hem rule for the bottoms: 2 bodies in the walk;
     - a wider gusset (3.2 cm): 2 bodies, 14.4 mm.
6. **`garment_checks`**, the helpers content tracks call:
   - **`motion(assets, description, clip, frames)`** samples a starter clip frame by frame and measures three things:
     - **Pop.** A garment corner's jolt (its second difference) must stay within 1.25 times the most the skin within 10 cm jolts, plus 2 mm.
     - **Tear.** Corners made at one place must stay within 0.2 mm.
     - **Hair.** No hair corner more than 1 mm inside the skin of the head.

     The Run is sampled to frame 35, where the clip, played alone, stops.
   - **`footwear(assets, description, stance)`** measures two things:
     - **Through.** A foot corner the shoe covers at rest is out of it when the ray out along its normal, which met the shoe then, meets no shoe in the pose.
     - **Tread.** The tread is the shoe's downward faces within 1 cm of its lowest point. At rest it must be within 2 mm of the floor and flat within 2 mm.
   - **Results.** Both return reports. `problems()` gives `Problem`s, and `unrecorded(problems, records)` leaves out those a `Record` (an open defect of a draft) allows.
   - **How I set the pop rule.** I tried three rules before this one, and each was wrong:
     - the jolt against the nearest skin's: it counted cloth standing off the skin;
     - a spike over the frames either side: it took negative excess as spikes;
     - drift from an affine fit of the nearest skin: it failed across joints.

     With the rule now, the underwear shows 0.00 mm in every clip. The checks' own test (`the_motion_checks_catch_a_pop_a_tear_and_hair_in_the_head`) covers the measures.
7. **The fit gate:**
   - checks layered pairs: the bra under the test tee, and the stylized cargo pants and sports top under the stylized tee;
   - leaves shoes to the footwear check (the old rule counted the foot over a shoe's inner floor);
   - has a test of the ratchet itself.

## Open issues

1. **The test tee in the walk** (54 bodies, 30 mm).
   - **Cause:** the chest at the front of the armpit and the inside of the upper arm come out where the arm hangs against the side. In the fit's own poses the arms hanging alone (no swing) fail on 108 corners of the masculine average body.
   - **What didn't work:**
     - the swing correctives and the posed fit's following of them;
     - fit poses closer to the clip;
     - (in wave 3) pushing out in the pose.
   - **A diagnostic:** I tried a ray rule (count a skin corner only if its ray out along its normal meets no cloth). It brought the walk to 22 bodies at 10 mm and the arms raised to 0. It shows that most of the cases are skin under folded cloth, but I didn't adopt it: it would change the gate's rule.
   - **What would fix it:** a garment modelled with the arms down, cloth proxies or deformer maps (ADR-0035), or region hiding of the armpit's skin under sleeves (a finer region map, `regions.py`).
2. **The boxer briefs' gusset** (13.3 mm). One skin corner by the gusset's edge on the masculine heavy-soft body is out even on the plain body (13 mm), but the gate counts it as covered only there. `push_out`'s skin-under-cloth check doesn't find it, with either hem rule. Next step: measure that corner in `push_out` directly.
3. **The test tee's armpit pops in motion** (4.1 mm walk, 11.7 mm run): large copies of the swing correctives from the posed fit (the follow puts up to 12 cm into a corrective).
4. **The stylized clothes are not refitted** (item 5 of the task):
   - the underwear's skin blotches;
   - the tee's hem crumpled over the waistband;
   - the tee's collar case (the rule counts the collar's rolled-in inner band as covering the neck);
   - the muscular feminine shoulder (9 mm);
   - the sports top through the tee;
   - the cargo pants' crotch pop;
   - the sneakers' tread and walk;
   - four hairstyles inside the head.

   All are recorded as drafts' open defects with numbers.
5. **The fine alignment assumes even ease.** On the wave-4 tee (shoulders and collar on the skin) a garment 5% small and 3 cm high isn't moved. The test uses `TestTeeEvenEase.obj`.
6. **Visible defects of the test tee** (a draft): the wavy collar edge, the dark underarm flap with the arms raised on the feminine bodies, and the crumpled sleeves.
7. The deepest fold corner, the armpit's apex when the arm hangs, isn't reached by any corrective.
