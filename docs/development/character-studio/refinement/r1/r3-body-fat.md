# Hand-back: R3 body fat (RA3), branch `r3-body-fat`

RA3's half of R3 that needs no interface: fat that looks like fat (plan 11.5 R3; the baseline's H6; OPEN-08). Branched from `character-creator-stage0` (5b650d5d). Pictures and what I saw in them: [../r3/README.md](../r3/README.md).

## What it does

- **`fat` at 1 reads as a heavier person on both frames.** A new identity shape per frame, `<Frame>_Fat_Soft`, driven by `fat` from 0 to 1 (all of it from `muscle` 0 up, half at `muscle` -1, whose own MakeHuman corner already carries a belly). It is MakeHuman's own CC0 targets, carried onto the body by the existing transfer (`assets/characters/transfer/`, `--method filtered`), mixed by fixed weights in `shapes.SOFT`; nothing is sculpted by script and nothing was fitted to a measure:
  - masculine: `bodyshapes-elvs-man-thyroid` 0.4 (fat over the whole body), `bodyshapes-elvs-man-adrenal` 0.3 (belly and flanks), `neck-double-incr` 0.6 (under the chin);
  - feminine: `bodyshapes-elvs-fem-ovary` 0.5 (hips, thighs, buttocks), `bodyshapes-elvs-fem-liver` 0.35 (lower belly), `l-` and `r-upperarm-fat-incr` 0.7, `neck-double-incr` 0.5.
  - It is a fat shape, so `shapes.bony` holds it back over the collarbones, elbows, wrists, kneecaps, shins, ankles, hands and feet as it does the others, and `shapes.face_hold` keeps it off the lids and lips.
- **Why the old `fat` was not fat.** `fat` walks MakeHuman's weight axis from where each body sits on its grid; the masculine base sits at muscle 0.45. At average muscle MakeHuman's heaviest body is 7 mm deeper at the belly than its average one (bulk); at its least muscle, 108 mm. So `fat` 1 alone thickened an athlete.
- **The slight masculine body loses its small belly.** `Masculine_Belly_Flat`, the product of `muscle` and `fat` both going to -1: MakeHuman's `stomach-pregnant-decr` at 1. The feminine frame gets none (`shapes.FLAT`).
- **Region fat is the same fat.** `fat_<region>`'s step to the heavy and soft corner now includes that corner's `Fat_Soft` share.
- **Measured on the built model** (against the default; before in brackets): masculine `fat` 1: belly 62 mm forward (8), waist 72 mm wider (21), hips 59 (13), a thigh 24 (8), buttocks 12 mm back (5); feminine: belly 37 (12), waist 75 (27), hips 65 (21), thigh 45 (14), buttocks 29 (5). Fat under the chin and jaw moves up to 20 mm (masculine), 9 mm (feminine); the cheeks under 6 mm. Slight masculine belly 25 mm behind the slim body's (10 before).
- **Kept apart** (the build's rest-pose check, `Realistic.build.json`): masculine heavy and soft 331 vertices to 0.21 (221 to 0.26 before), heavy and muscular 493 to 0.33 (431 to 0.41), slight 76 to 0.80 (100); feminine heavy and soft 123 to 0.80 (0), heavy and muscular 316 to 0.51 (94 to 0.80), slight 124 to 0.80 (0). All 0 crossings left.

## Open issues

1. **Heavy and muscular** still has a bodybuilder's arms, shoulders and trapezius (OPEN-08); it now has a belly. Taming it means changing the muscle corners, which wasn't this task.
2. **The face under fat**: only the jaw line and under the chin fill; cheeks don't (the face hold). Fuller cheeks would need `head-fat-incr` (read, not used) and RA2's agreement, since it reaches the face.
3. **Feminine thigh bulge**: `fem-ovary`'s outer upper thigh is the sharpest part of the change and throws a shadow line under the hard light. Lowering its weight trades it against hip fullness.
4. **The athletic normal map** still draws abdominal lines on every heavy belly (H5, CO7's).
5. **The kept-apart cut is larger**: feminine heavy and muscular now scales every shape of the frame down to 0.51 over 316 vertices (armpits and inner thighs), against 0.80 over 94.
6. **Limb correspondence** stays 1 to 3 cm loose (OPEN-08's last point); not touched.
7. **`makehuman.sha256` hashes CRLF checkouts** (Windows with `core.autocrlf`), as the spike's 33 did: a Linux checkout matches none of the 41. Fixing it means re-hashing all 41 from the blobs; left as it was so the record stays consistent.

My change alters no benchmark, so I reverted that output to keep the diff to my work; RA6 or the lead should regenerate.
9. The package build is not bit-exact against `main`'s committed files (tiny drifts in `Realistic.build.json`, 560 pixels of `RealisticRegions.png`), from the build environment, not from this change.
