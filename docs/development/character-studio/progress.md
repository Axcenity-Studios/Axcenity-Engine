- **Why Paul couldn't see the realistic body in Character Studio.** Two reasons. - **Paul's decisions** (the two lines CO5 and CO6 left open):
  - *The raised shoulder* is held by its **ring area** at 150° (0.929 / 0.82 measured; `at_least` 0.92 / 0.81 in `assets/characters/realistic/qa/thresholds.json`), not its local volume (0.463 / 0.513, still measured and reported), the lead's recommendation: the local volume rewards an inflated cap. The 0.8 local-volume target and the 0.54 floor are gone; the 90° targets stay.
  - *The eye check* signs distances properly. `qa_scene.signed_distance` signed a point by the normal of whichever face the tree returned. Now a `Geo` signs by the angle-weighted pseudo-normal (`qa/pseudo_normals.py`, plain numpy, `test_pseudo_normals.py`): of the face, the edge (both faces) or the corner its nearest point is on, with corners welded by position. A first version found that point's region from the tree's single-precision nearest point with a tolerance, read points on an edge as inside the face, and counted more, not fewer (3 and 1); the diagnostic [stage3/eye_points.py](stage3/eye_points.py) showed it (a point 2.3 mm outside read 0.06 mm inside by the face's plane). The region is now worked out again in double precision (Ericson's closest point on a triangle), and the three points CO6 flagged measure 4.7, 2.5 and 2.4 mm outside, as the CO6 lead had found by hand; four rays' parity agrees.
