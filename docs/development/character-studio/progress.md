## 2026-11-16: the lowered shoulder (a CO5 defect)

- **Seen:** Paul, looking at the realistic body in Character Studio: "the shoulders look strange, as if they are deformed". At rest (the 45 degree A-pose the body is skinned in) they're right; the starter Idle hangs the arms 11 degrees from hanging (Walk 6 to 22, measured on the retargeted takes), 34 below rest, and CO5's correctives only correct raising the arm (all four read 0 in the idle). Plain skinning squared off the cap and raised a ridge beside the neck.
- **Built:** `Corrective_Shoulder_Lowered_Left/Right`, a delta mush of the arm 10 degrees from hanging, made on their own (not on top of Level and Raised), driven from 0 to 35 degrees about the raise axis reversed (`correctives.py` `STAGES`, `anim` `CORRECTIVES`). A QA pose `shoulder_abd_10`. **Harness bug found on the way:** `qa_scene._shoulder` passed a signed turn to `bend(..., toward=UP)`, and `toward` sets the direction, so a target below the rest pose raised the arm instead (the first build of the shape was made in the arm raised to 80 degrees; caught by reading the corrective weights in that pose). It now passes the size and the direction.
- **Measured** ([lowered-shoulder/](lowered-shoulder/)): the arm hanging, the shoulder's local volume 1.125 / 1.093 before (masculine / feminine), 1.024 / 0.986 after; ring area 0.997 / 0.988 before, 0.907 / 0.962 after. New limits: local volume at most 1.03 / 0.99, ring area at least 0.9 / 0.96. Every other limit holds; the 90 and 150 degree figures are unchanged.
- **Seen in the engine** (`AXCENITY_CAPTURE_POSE=idle`, the old library against the new): the squared cap, the peak at the top of the trapezius and the ridge beside the neck are gone on both frames and the heavy body, in front, three-quarter, back and hard light ([lowered-shoulder/idle-before-after-*.jpg](lowered-shoulder/)). ## 2026-11-16: Stage 3 done, and landed on main

- **Why Paul couldn't see the realistic body in Character Studio.** Two reasons. (1) Stage 3 never reached `main`: #407 to #411 were stacked (each based on the one before), and each was merged into its base branch after that branch had already merged, so the work stopped on `co5-body-parameters`. Every PR head is in that branch, which merges cleanly into `main`; it lands as one PR. Don't merge a stack top-down again: merge bottom-up into `main`, retargeting each PR to `main` before merging it. (2) Even on the branch, the workspace edits format 1 and draws only the stylized humanoid built into the engine; the realistic package isn't built in (`RealisticParts.glb` is 32 MB). That is CO12, which Stage 4 starts with.
- **Paul's decisions** (the two lines CO5 and CO6 left open):
  - *The raised shoulder* is held by its **ring area** at 150° (0.929 / 0.82 measured; `at_least` 0.92 / 0.81 in `assets/characters/realistic/qa/thresholds.json`), not its local volume (0.463 / 0.513, still measured and reported), the lead's recommendation: the local volume rewards an inflated cap. The 0.8 local-volume target and the 0.54 floor are gone; the 90° targets stay.
  - *The eye check* signs distances properly. `qa_scene.signed_distance` signed a point by the normal of whichever face the tree returned. Now a `Geo` signs by the angle-weighted pseudo-normal (`qa/pseudo_normals.py`, plain numpy, `test_pseudo_normals.py`): of the face, the edge (both faces) or the corner its nearest point is on, with corners welded by position. A first version found that point's region from the tree's single-precision nearest point with a tolerance, read points on an edge as inside the face, and counted more, not fewer (3 and 1); the diagnostic [stage3/eye_points.py](stage3/eye_points.py) showed it (a point 2.3 mm outside read 0.06 mm inside by the face's plane). The region is now worked out again in double precision (Ericson's closest point on a triangle), and the three points CO6 flagged measure 4.7, 2.5 and 2.4 mm outside, as the CO6 lead had found by hand; four rays' parity agrees.
- **Measured** ([stage3/](stage3/README.md)): `measure.py --package realistic --check`: every limit holds; only the 90° shoulder targets print as not met (0.847 / 0.88 against 0.853 / 0.992). `--only sliders --check`: 0 new crossings in 10,082 bodies on each frame, 0 parameter ends with eye or teeth points in the flesh on either.
- **Tests** on the merged branch: `cargo nextest run --workspace --no-fail-fast`: 4915 run, 4911 passed, 2 failed, 2 timed out. Run alone: `collab` `random_edits_over_a_bad_network_end_the_same_everywhere` and `editor` `every_genre_templates_tests_pass_in_the_tests_panel_as_on_the_command_line` pass (load); `mesh-edit`'s 500k sculpt timing fails at the edge of its budget (Clay Strips, 95% within 32.9 ms), as in CO4 and CO6, and Stage 3 doesn't touch `mesh-edit`; `editor` `each_mode_draws_hello_graphics_its_own_way_and_lit_comes_back` ("Path Traced looks like Ray Hits") fails on `main` too on this machine's GPU. `python3 -m unittest test_pseudo_normals`: 6 passed.
