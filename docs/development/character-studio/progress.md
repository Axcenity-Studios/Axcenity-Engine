## 2026-11-24 (third entry): Paul's findings document, and the studio made usable (branch `character-studio-wardrobe-fit`)

Paul sent "Feature Requests & Improvements" (40 pages, mostly close-ups from Character Studio) with reference pictures (nose charts, head-morph trees, sculpt markers): the highlight glitches on clothes; clothes vanish when the body is adjusted; the face needs far more depth, with markers round its outline; the garments are torn, holed, let the body through and are heavy; hide the body under clothes; and the layout is cumbersome. One lead session on the garment fit, three specialists in the same tree: layout, highlight, face.

- **What it was** (each measured or reproduced before anything changed):
  - **Tears.** On every fitted garment, corners at one place (the two sides of a texture seam) moved apart in the shoulder correctives: 4 cm to over a metre at full weight, and neighbouring corners of the bomber jacket up to 22 cm. The posed fit worked corner by corner from each corner's own binding, and a loose corner bound to far skin swings with it. Idle lowers the arms from the rest pose, so the tears showed standing still.
  - **Holes.** The source meshes come with 5 to 49 small open rings each; taking off the inner wall, thinning and cutting strings and webs added more, and nothing closed any. A fitted garment had 5 to 59 open rings where 3 to 5 are openings.
  - **Arms outside sleeves.** A sleeve is turned to the rest pose as one straight piece; the puffer's is bent at the elbow, and from there down lay 2 to 14 cm above the arm's line. A sleeve modelled hanging shares a wall with the side: cut apart, it was open along its underside.
  - **Black patches** were the underwear through holes and coarse cloth: fitted by `shapes`, it had no place on a cover map. **Skin** showed because the cover map hid only skin whose weights matched the cloth's: 566 of the 3,349 triangles under the bomber jacket.
  - **Heavy and faceted.** A garment had about three corners a place (a normal per triangle corner), each skinned and carrying every moving shape: 460,000 to 700,000 shape moves an item.
  - **Clothes vanishing** was the try-on: a clicked tile was shown but not worn until **Wear**, and any edit or page change ended it. Worn clothes never dropped (now a test).
  - **The highlight** was triangles of the garment tested against a low-resolution depth buffer with a millimetre's tolerance, outlined along the garment's own triangle edges.
- **Built:**
  - **The fit** (`crates/avatar/src/fit/`; [architecture.md](architecture.md) §9, ADR-0044 and ADR-0045 amended): the posed fit seam-safe and, for intake garments, eased, smoothed and limited (`posed.rs`); `fit/mend.rs` (holes filled, the intake's sleeve cuts closed, the fit's web cuts closed without making webs); `intake/centre.rs` (sleeves and legs round their limbs); `intake/weld.rs` (smooth normals, merged corners, wrongly wound triangles turned); floating scraps out (`conform::tidy`); cuffs and hems weighted to the forearm and shin (`parts::held_joints`); cover maps proved in the fit poses (`cover::Proof`); underwear hidden under clothes (`assets`: `RegionHiding::skin`, `Model::with_skin_from`, `without_well_covered`; `gameplay`'s applier). `IntakeReport` says how open the cloth was after each step; `import_generated.py --reports <folder>` keeps what the tool said of each garment.

## 2026-11-24 (second entry): Paul's review of the wardrobe in Character Studio, and ADR-0045 (branch `character-studio-wardrobe-fit`)

Paul dressed a character in Character Studio and reported: clothes broken and malformed on the character, not conforming or wrapping; clothes not separated by gender; adjusting the body a headache once dressed ("the adjustment is on the character not the clothed item"); two sets of clothes worn at once; no way to click a garment off; and the stand looking anxious. He asked for research, the garments made again, and a workflow that works across garment types. One lead session; a research agent wrote [character-clothing-workflow.md](../../research/character-clothing-workflow.md).

- **What it was** (each reproduced in the capture harness before anything changed):
  - The grey patches on the legs were the joggers worn **under** the jumpsuit: the jumpsuit took only the `top` slot. The pink blotches on the chest were the studio's hover tint, drawn on the skin and shown through any cloth within 1.5 cm of it.
  - Garments imported apart crossed at the waist on every body and in every pose (a top's hem through a waistband, a tank through a jacket), and no skin was hidden under any of them.
  - Sleeves modelled hanging and turned to the rest pose hung under the arm like wings; cut edges were ragged; cloth joined under the arm tore into strips when the arm lifted.
  - Idle held the A-pose's palms forward on lowered arms.
- **Built** ([ADR-0045](../../../wiki/Architecture/Architecture-Decisions/ADR-0045-What-Is-Worn-Over-Skin-Or-Cloth-Hides-It-By-A-Cover-Map.md), [architecture.md](architecture.md) §8 and §9):
  - **Cover maps.** Every import writes the item's `cover_map` (over the body's texture coordinates: skin to leave out, and skin under which cloth worn beneath is left out, both set back from openings) and each corner's place on the skin (`_AXCENITY_SKIN_UV`, `Primitive::skin_uvs`). `resolve` orders the worn garments (`ItemManifest::wearing_order`) and the applier draws the body and each garment worn under another from a model with the covered triangles left out (`RegionHiding` with `covers`; `Model::without_covered` sinks the edge triangles' covered corners). `cargo run -p axcenity-avatar --release --example cover_map -- <library>` makes an item's map again without fitting it again (12 s for the 32).
  - **The intake conforms and tidies** (`intake/conform.rs`, `fit/parts.rs`): cloth shared between the trunk, arms and legs, each patch drawn in round its own part by a per-garment class (`conform` in `garments.json`, after Paul's fitting profiles), skin weights by the same shares, a skirt hung from the hips, scraps and teeth off the cut edges and hems evened.
  - **Slots and frames** (`garments.json`, no new manifest field): one-pieces and dresses fill `bottom`; the suits are `outerwear` that fills `bottom`; overalls are mid-layer `bottom`; each garment is tagged `masculine`, `feminine` or `unisex`, the first two with a `frame` limit.
  - **Character Studio**: the Clothing page's **Cut for** (This body, Masculine, Feminine, All; the online wardrobe too); a click on a worn tile takes it off and a click on the tile being tried on lets it go; a feature's tint and outline are drawn on the garment over it and handles are hidden only by skin, so the body is adjusted dressed (`direct.rs`, `WornMap`).
  - **Idle at ease** (`assets/characters/humanoid/animations.py`, `standing` and `turn_forearms`): forearms turned 25 degrees so the palms face the thighs, softer elbows and fingers, a little give in the back and neck; held by Idle, the gaits, the crouch and the strafes, and where the gestures start and end. `HumanoidAnimations.glb` rebuilt with Blender 5.2.2 (the other files of the build left as they were) and copied to the humanoid sample.
- **What went wrong on the way** (each seen in a capture or caught by a test, not predicted):
  - The first conform moved each corner toward its nearest skin: it crumpled the polo and crushed its collar. It now shortens a patch's mean clearance and smooths the moves, part by part.
  - Cutting webs at 2.5 and then 3 times left holes in sleeves that show with the arms down, and slits in every long skirt (a stride draws a skirt out). Now 5 times, never in a skirt.
  - Evening the hems drew each loop in onto the body (the skirt intake test caught it): two steps a round, in and back out.
  - Arms hanging nearer the side than before hid the waist handles from the front (two handle tests): the upper arm stays where the old Idle had it.
  - The cover map first hid skin under any cloth out along its normal. The fit gate found hidden skin with nothing over it on the test tee (the inner arm, "covered" by the shirt's side). Skin is now hidden only under cloth that moves with it (0.8 of its skin weights shared), never in a crease, the whole triangle set back at least 2.5 cm from an opening. Applying the same strictness to cloth worn beneath brought the waist crossing back, so cloth keeps the looser rule.
  - **The gate's hole rule changed**: hidden skin is a hole where no cloth lies over it (`fit::uncovered`), not wherever it is outside the cloth. Cloth sunk a little under hidden skin is what hiding is for. Region hiding at import keeps the stricter rule.
- **Result** (32 garments imported in 12 min 39 s, seven at a time; 31 outfits captured at rest from three sides, five more through the body suite on earlier builds of the day; looked at at contact-sheet size and a few full size):
