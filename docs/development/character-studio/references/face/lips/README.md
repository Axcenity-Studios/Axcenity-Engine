## Anatomy

The lips are a ring of muscle covered by skin outside and wet lining inside, with the red of the lip (vermilion) between. The red meets the skin at a fine raised border. On the upper lip the border dips in the middle and rises to two peaks (crista philtri), the Cupid's bow; above it two ridges run up to the nose with a groove between them, the philtrum. The upper lip's red has a central swelling and thinner sides; the lower lip's red is fuller and has two soft swellings side by side. The lips meet at stomion and at the corners (cheilion), where several face muscles join in a firm knot just outside the corner. Below the lower lip a groove (sublabiale) separates it from the chin. The lips rest on the teeth, so how far the teeth stand forward sets how far the lips do.

## Sensible limits

| Control | Limit | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Mouth width | ±4.8 mm a corner (as built) | ±2.3 SD. The corners should stay inside the lines dropped from the pupils (62 to 65 mm apart) except on a stylised face. |
| Mouth height | ±4.5 mm (as built) | About 2 SD of philtrum length. This control already is philtrum length. |
| Upper and lower fullness | ±4 mm of red height each (as built) | 2 SD. Opposing points of the two lips must move alike so the mouth stays closed (`assets/characters/realistic/face.py:1533-1534`). |
| Cupid's bow (new) | ±1.5 mm of peak height | By eye; under 1 SD of philtrum width. |
| Lip corners (new) | ±2 mm up or down | By eye. Keep it apart from the smile and frown expression shapes and limit the sum, or a turned-up mouth doubles when it smiles. |
| Mouth projection (new) | ±3 mm | By eye. The teeth must move with the lips, as they do for mouth height (`assets/characters/realistic/face.py:1544-1548`). |
| Left against right corner | Within 1 mm for a natural face; 2.2 mm for the asymmetry benchmark | People notice 3 mm. |
