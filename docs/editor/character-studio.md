# Character Studio

Character Studio has two places: a **workspace** of its own, opened from the start screen with no project (`docs/research/character-studio.md` CS2, decision D9), for making and dressing characters; and the **tab** in the Game Editor, for a character's face.

## The workspace

Code: `crates/editor/src/character_workspace.rs` (the description, presets, undo, the preview World, the library, saving) with `character_workspace/ui.rs` (the layout and the pages drawn from the manifest) and `stage.rs` (camera, lighting, backdrop); the editor's side, which opens it and asks before leaving, is `app/character_workspace.rs`; the start page is `start/characters.rs`. The contract is [architecture.md](../development/character-studio/architecture.md) (sections 1, 5 to 7 and 15); Character Studio overhaul CO12 moved the workspace onto it.

- **Opening it.** The start screen's sidebar (or top bar) has **Character Studio**, after Projects. Its page has New Character (the realistic male default), a card per preset and the saved characters with Open. `axcenity-editor --character-studio` opens straight into it. It needs no project; while it's open the Game Editor's menus aren't shown, and the window is titled with the character's name (`*` while unsaved).
- **Its files** are in the editor's data folder, `Character Studio/` (on Windows `%APPDATA%\Axcenity Editor\data\Character Studio`): the default humanoid's files (`project::add_default_humanoid`), the **character library** in `assets/characters/` and the saved characters, `characters/<name>.character.json`. The library is the built-in stylized package (`add_character_library`) plus every package the engine ships (`add_character_packages`, which copies a package's manifests and the files they name when they're missing or their size changed, and writes the merged catalogue). The shipped library is found by `find_character_library`: `assets/characters/` (with a `catalogue.json`) in the folder the editor was started from, in a folder above the editor's program (an installed editor has it beside itself: `packaging/stage.py` puts it in every payload) or in a debug build's source tree. Without it only the stylized body is offered, with a notice.
- **The layout.** A toolbar (Home, the workspace's name, the character's name to type in, "Not saved", Undo, Redo, Reset, Save), the categories down the left (Presets, Body, Face, Hair, Clothing, Stage), the viewport in the middle, the category's controls on the right, and a status line (where characters are saved, what was saved, what failed). The side panels narrow before the viewport does.
- **The character** is a `Description` (format 2) on a base model of the library: the realistic one. The stylized body isn't offered for new characters (`offers_base`; it stays in the library for games and old files), so it has no presets and no chip on the Body page; a saved character on it still opens, and then the Body page shows the body chips so it can move onto the realistic body. The preview is the Character Runtime's: the default character (`project::default_character`) in a preview World of the workspace's own, with a round floor, and the description applied by `gameplay::description::apply_description`, exactly as a game applies it (identity baked into derived models, the base model's own parts, the items worn). The engine's `Animators` pose it each frame, so it idles. A change is applied at once; while a slider is dragged, at most every 120 ms and again when the drag ends (applying, with the bake, takes about 6 ms on the realistic body in a debug build).
- **Presets** (plain data, `presets()`), all on the realistic body: Male (the default), Female, Athletic male, Athletic female, Heavyset male and Heavyset female. Picking one keeps the name; a preset whose body the library hasn't isn't shown. A preset that sets a parameter with choices wears what that choice wears. **Body**: the manifest's `body` groups, a collapsible section each with a slider per parameter, except a parameter with `choices`, which is a chip per choice: the realistic body's **Body type** is Male or Female (`frame` 0 or 1), not a slider. Picking one is one undo step and wears that choice's `items` (Male: boxer briefs; Female: briefs and a sports bra), taking off the items the other choice names but not what was picked by hand. A value between the choices (from a script or an older file) is taken to the nearest when the character is edited. **Face**: the manifest's `face` groups the same way; picking Face frames the face, and Body frames the whole character again. On both pages **Symmetry** (on by default) shows a mirrored pair as one slider ("Left ear protrusion" is "Ear protrusion") that sets both, and **Advanced** shows the parameters the manifest marks advanced. A drag is one undo step; double-click a slider for its default. **Hair** and **Clothing**: the manifest's slots of that category, each a row with the items of the catalogue that fit this body and the slot's empty choice ("Bald", "None"); a slot nothing fits isn't shown. The realistic body's items so far are its underwear (Underwear: boxer briefs or briefs; Underwear top: a sports bra; `assets/characters/realistic/underwear.py`); its hair and clothes are CO10 and CO11. The Face page's Brows group has brow height, arch, length (the brows' card drawn in or carried down the temple) and ridge. **Stage** (not saved with the character): lighting (Studio, Daylight, Sunset, Night: the preview's time of day), backdrop (Grey, Light, Dark, or the sky) and the views.
- **The viewport**: the character stands on the floor (the stylized starter clips play on the realistic body through humanoid roles, from its own rest pose, so its feet stay down and its shoulders keep their own line: `docs/rendering/animation.md`); drag to turn, right- or middle-drag to pan, scroll to zoom, double-click for the full body again; the view buttons over its top are Full body, Face, Front, Side and Back.
- **Undo** is the workspace's own: each change of the description is a step (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z), Reset is one (the body's default character, keeping the name). **Save** (Ctrl+S) writes the file; Home with unsaved changes asks Save, Don't Save or Cancel.
- **The character file** is the engine-wide description, format 2 ([architecture.md](../development/character-studio/architecture.md) §5): `name`, `base`, `parameters` by the manifest's IDs, `items`, `materials`. A format 1 file (CS2's `Look`: build, muscle and a part per slot) opens on the stylized body wearing what it wore and saves as format 2. Values out of a parameter's range are brought in and parameters the body hasn't are dropped; a file on a body the library hasn't opens the default character with a notice; a newer format is refused with a message.

## The tab

Character Studio's tab is a document tab beside the Scene (View > Character Studio, the ribbon's Panels menu, or Ctrl+K "Character Studio"). It shows the selected character's face in a preview of its own, with the **Face** workspace (facial animation F6, decision D9 in [research/facial-animation.md](../research/facial-animation.md)). The default-humanoid work adds its body and customization workspaces to the same tab.

Code: `crates/editor/src/character_studio.rs` (state, preview, the tab) with `character_studio/rig.rs`, `expressions.rs` and `lip_sync.rs` (one per section); the editor's side, which applies what the tab asks for, is `app/character_studio.rs`.

## What it shows

- It follows the selection: a FaceControls in a MeshPart, a MeshPart (with its FaceControls if it has one), or a Model or folder with a FaceControls in a MeshPart inside (`face_target`). With something else selected it keeps the last character.
- The preview draws a copy of the MeshPart alone, at the origin and unturned, posed at rest with its face set by `anim::pose_face` (blend shape weights and eye and jaw turns through the matched profile). The camera looks at the head bone (`anim::face_point`; a model without bones is framed on its top) from the way the body faces. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, double-click to frame again.
- What the face shows depends on the section: the FaceControls' own values (Rig), the expression (Expressions), or the visemes at the play head (Lip sync).

## Rig

- **Profile**: the FaceControls' `Profile` (Best fit, a built-in one, or one of the project's `*.face.json`), set as one undo step. The line under it names the profile matched and how many of the 48 controls move something on this model.
- **Check**: what doesn't fit (`anim::check_face`), errors first; after six, the rest fold.
- **Controls**: the FaceControls' 48 controls as sliders, grouped (eyes, eyebrows, mouth and lips, cheeks and nose, jaw and tongue), with a filter. A slider is an edit like Properties' (one undo step per drag). Controls that move nothing on the model are greyed. Without a FaceControls the sliders are off; expressions and lip syncs still preview.
- **Key in Animation** selects the FaceControls and opens the Animation tab, where its controls are rows to key in a clip (F4b). That is the face's timeline: keys, curves and playback are the Animation tab's.
- **Profile mapping** (folded at first): Edit mapping copies the matched profile, keeping only the shapes the model has. Each of the model's blend shapes can be given to one control with a weight (`map_shape`: a shape belongs to one control at a time). The Jaw, LeftEye and RightEye bones can be found by name, set to none, or named; the eye and jaw limits are in degrees. Save profile writes `assets/faces/<name>.face.json` (one undo step) and makes it the FaceControls' `Profile` (another). The preview and the check follow the draft while it's edited.

## Expressions

- The project's expressions, then the built-in ones it doesn't replace (`anim::expression_names`); built-in ones are greyed, and saving one makes the project's own copy.
- **Preview at** scales it; **Blend with** another expression mixes the two by **Blend amount** (preview only).
- The expression's own intensity, transition and the controls it sets (add, change, take out). Save expression writes `assets/faces/expressions/<name>.expression.json` (one undo step); a game plays it with `face:SetExpression("<name>")`.

## Lip sync

- Open any `*.lipsync.json` in the project (hidden folders and `target`, `build` and `node_modules` are skipped; the arrows list the files again).
- **Make from** makes one beside a sound (`.ogg`, `.wav`, `.mp3`, `.flac`: from how it sounds) or phoneme timings (`.lab`, `.tsv`, `.txt`: `start end phoneme` per line), as the Console's `lipsync` does, saves it (one undo step) and opens it.
- The timeline shows the lip sync's sound as a waveform over its length, and its visemes as blocks, each until the next. Click the waveform to move the play head; click a block to pick it, drag it to retime it (kept between its neighbours, `retime`); double-click to add a viseme there (`add_key`). The picked viseme's row changes which viseme it is, its width (weight) and its time, or deletes it; Add at play head adds one.
- Play plays the visemes on the face through the FaceControls' `VisemeMap`, `LipSyncSmoothing` and `LipSyncAnticipation` (or the defaults), as a game would. The sound doesn't play in the tab.
- Length changes the lip sync's duration. Save writes it (one undo step); "Not saved" shows until then.

## Tests

The workspace: `character_workspace/tests.rs` (the realistic body offered and shown with its parts and underwear, the stylized one not offered; Male or Female as a choice that wears its own underwear; a slider re-baking the preview during a drag, one undo step; symmetry; switching body and undo; a format 1 file opening on the stylized body and saving as format 2; dressing in the realistic underwear, undo, save, reopen and reset; every preset sound against the shipped library; every page drawn on both bodies, with `AXCENITY_SNAPSHOTS` the Body and Face pages drawn for real; the views; a saved character applied in a game drawn from the same models as the preview; the preview rendered offscreen for both realistic frames and the stylized body), `app/character_workspace.rs` (the start screen lists it, a preset opens it without the Game Editor's menus, leaving asks to save, a saved file opens clean) and `project` `the_shipped_packages_are_copied_beside_the_built_in_one`.

The tab: `character_studio/tests.rs` (what a selection shows, mapping shapes, retiming and adding visemes, the waveform, lip sync and expression previews, file names and listing) and `app/character_studio.rs` (the tab on Remy in hello-character: sliders with undo, saving an expression, a profile and a lip sync, Make from a WAV, Key in Animation). `AXCENITY_SNAPSHOTS=<folder>` saves each section, preview drawn, as `character-studio-*.png`. `anim`'s `face_tests.rs` covers `pose_face`, `face_point`, `FaceRig::moves` and `CompiledVisemes::apply`.
