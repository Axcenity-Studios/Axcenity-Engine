# Scatter pane

Read this when changing the Scatter pane, scatter palettes (`.axscatter`) or the Scatter brush op. The terrain side (painted foliage, `foliage.json`, drawing, collide) is [../terrain.md](../terrain.md).

- **What it is**: a dockable pane (`Pane::Scatter`, `scatter_pane.rs`; its app side `app/scatter_pane.rs`) for painting a weighted random mix of plants, props and models onto Terrain as painted foliage. It replaced the Terrain Studio's Foliage tool. It opens from the ribbon's **Models** group (**Scatter**, beside Import… and Model Studio), universal search (Ctrl+K "Scatter", also found by "foliage") and View > Panels; by default it docks as a tab beside Properties.
- **Palette**: a list of items, each a `FoliageSource` (a built-in prop or plant, or a project model under `assets/models`) with its icon and name and: **on** (off: not placed, not erased by Shift), **Weight** (its share of the mix; the pane shows each item's share as a percentage of the enabled items' total, `brush::shares`), **Size** (smallest and largest scale, picked at random between, kept in order), **Collide** (Solid, or visual only), **Rotation** (random turn about the up axis, 0 to 360 degrees), **Align to slope** (0 upright to 1 square to the ground: `FoliageInstance::lean`) and **Sink** (studs lowered into the ground: `FoliageInstance::sink`). New items start on, weight 1, × 0.85 to 1.15, visual only, 360°, upright. **Add from Assets** turns on picking (`Picking::Scatter` in `asset_browser.rs`): while the pane is open, a click on a prop or model tile in the Assets panel adds it (once; tiles already in the palette are highlighted); a global asset is copied into the project first. The trash button takes an item out.
- **Brush**: **Size** (radius, 0.5 to 256 studs; `[` and `]` while armed), **Density** (items per 100 square studs, 0.1 to 200; the brush op takes per square stud, so the app divides by 100), **Spacing** (studs kept between a new item and any painted foliage, so strokes over the same ground fill gaps instead of piling up; 0 lets them touch) and **Erase everything**. The brush is full strength with a fixed soft rim (30% of the radius) and ignores the Terrain Studio's masks and symmetry.
- **Painting**: **Paint** arms the brush in the viewport through the terrain-brush plumbing (`scatter_brush_view` gives the viewport its circle, coloured like the first enabled item; while armed, `output.terrain` goes to `scatter_pointer`). Drag scatters; Shift+drag erases the palette's enabled items under the brush, or with Erase everything any painted foliage. Escape, Paint again, closing the pane, Play or picking a terrain tool puts it down; arming it puts a held terrain tool down. A stroke paints onto the Terrain Studio's terrain (the selected terrain or the first); with no terrain the pane says "Scatter paints onto Terrain", Paint is off, and Open Terrain Studio is offered. It doesn't paint onto parts. Each stroke takes the next seed (the pane's seed starts from the clock), so a stroke is deterministic for its seed and path. One stroke is one undo step, "Scatter N Items" (how many it placed) or "Erase Scatter"; a stroke that changed nothing makes none.
- **The op** (`terrain/src/brush/scatter.rs`, `Operation::Scatter(Scatter)`): per stamp, samples under the brush get `density × cell area × brush weight` tries, each jittered within its cell, skipped outside the brush, on a hole, or within the spacing of painted foliage or another new item (a hash grid of the nearby instances), then an item is picked by weight and placed with its own scale, turn, collide, lean and sink. At most 256 items a stamp and `MAX_FOLIAGE_INSTANCES` in all. Held still, a Scatter stroke doesn't stamp again.
- **Files**: palettes are the project's `presets/<name>.axscatter` (beside world presets' `.axworld`; `project::scatter_palettes`): JSON with `format` (1), `name`, `density` (per 100 square studs), `spacing` and `items`, each item's `item` written like a ScatterRule `Models` entry (`prop:Crate`, `plant:Grass` or a model path; `FoliageSource::entry` and `from_entry`) and its settings by name. Every field but `item` has a default; an unknown item is left out; a newer format is refused; unreadable files are reported in Output. The pane has a palette dropdown, **New** (an empty "Palette 2"…), **Rename** (moves the file; a name already taken, ignoring case, gets a number) and **Delete** (removes the file; deleting the last leaves an empty "Palette"). Edits save on their own once the mouse is let go (`ScatterPane::dirty`, saved by `scatter_frame`), and before switching palettes or projects. A project without palettes starts with an empty "Palette", written when first edited.
- **MCP**: `terrain_edit`'s `foliage` action still takes one `item`, `density` (per square stud) and `erase`, and runs it as a one-item Scatter op (labels "Paint Tree Foliage", "Erase Tree Foliage").
- **Not yet**: painting onto parts; renaming or moving a model (Rename or Move…, Find References) doesn't see the palettes that name it.
- **Tests**: `scatter_pane/tests.rs` (items, shares, the no-terrain card, unique names), `app/scatter_pane_tests.rs` (the ribbon command, clicking tiles in the real Assets panel, saving and reopening, a stroke as one undo step, Shift and Erase everything, Escape, palette files), and the terrain and project tests named in [../terrain.md](../terrain.md) and `project/src/scatter_palettes.rs`.
