# Scatter pane

Read this when changing the Scatter pane, scatter palettes (`.axscatter`), the Scatter brush op, an item's masks or the decal brush. The terrain side (painted foliage, `foliage.json`, drawing, collide) is [../terrain.md](../terrain.md).

- **What it is**: a dockable pane (`Pane::Scatter`, `scatter_pane.rs`; its app side `app/scatter_pane.rs`) for painting a weighted random mix of plants, props and models onto Terrain as painted foliage. It replaced the Terrain Studio's Foliage tool. It opens from the ribbon's **Models** group (**Scatter**, beside Import… and Model Studio), universal search (Ctrl+K "Scatter", also found by "foliage") and View > Panels; by default it docks as a tab beside Properties.
- **Palette**: a list of items, each a `FoliageSource` (a built-in prop or plant, or a project model under `assets/models`) with its icon and name and: **on** (off: not placed, not erased by Shift), **Weight** (its share of the mix; the pane shows each item's share as a percentage of the enabled items' total, `brush::shares`), **Size** (smallest and largest scale, picked at random between, kept in order), **Collide** (Solid, or visual only), **Shadow** (Casts shadow, or none: `FoliageInstance::shadow`, saved in the palette as `"shadow": false` only when off and in the terrain's `foliage.json` the same way), **Rotation** (random turn about the up axis, 0 to 360 degrees), **Align to slope** (0 upright to 1 square to the ground: `FoliageInstance::lean`) and **Sink** (studs lowered into the ground: `FoliageInstance::sink`). New items start on, weight 1, × 0.85 to 1.15, visual only, 360°, upright, with every mask open. **Add from Assets** turns on picking (`Picking::Scatter` in `asset_browser.rs`): while the pane is open, a click on a prop or model tile in the Assets panel adds it (once; tiles already in the palette are highlighted); a global asset is copied into the project first. The trash button takes an item out.
- **Masks** (`terrain/src/brush/scatter_mask.rs`, `ScatterItem::mask`; folded under each item as **Masks**, its title counting the ones that are on, "Masks (2 on)"): where the brush places more or less of an item. Each is a number from 0 to 1 and they multiply (`ScatterMask::density`). **Noise** (0 off to 1: how much it thins the item between its patches) with **Scale** (studs across a patch, 1 to 2,000), **Threshold** (0 to 1: how much ground the patches leave bare; 0.5 is about half), **Contrast** (0 a fade across the whole patch, 1 a hard edge) and **Seed** (another number moves the patches): three octaves of the terrain crate's gradient noise by world position, so the patches stay put between strokes. **Slope** (the gentlest and steepest ground, degrees; 0 to 90 is open) and **Height** (a tick, then the lowest and highest ground in world Y; ticking starts from the terrain's own range): each end fades over 5 degrees (`SLOPE_FADE`) or 4 studs (`HEIGHT_FADE`) centred on it, and an end at 0, 90 or ±10,000 (`OPEN_HEIGHT`) is open. **Moisture** (-1 to 1) with **Reach** (studs, 1 to 256; default 16): `wetness` is worked out from the terrain's shape alone, the mean height of two rings round the spot (Reach and half of it away, eight points each) less the spot's own height, as a share of Reach: 0 on a rise, 0.5 on flat or evenly sloping ground, 1 in a hollow, saturating at 5% of Reach (`WET_RELIEF`: the middle of a wash 3 studs deep and 40 wide is about 0.96 with Reach 16). Above 0 the item keeps `1 - bias × (1 - wetness)` of its density (at 1: all in a hollow, half on the flat, none on a rise), below 0 the other way round, so one item gathers in washes and another on dry rises. There is no flow or rainfall simulation behind it; Reach picks the size of hollow it sees. A stroke rolls a masked item's chance against a number made from the stroke's seed and the spot, not from the stroke's own random numbers, and before the spot takes up spacing: so open masks (the default) place exactly what the brush placed before masks existed, masking one item moves no other item, and ground a mask leaves bare stays free for the others.
- **Ground clutter**: the polygon button beside New makes a "Ground Clutter" palette (`ScatterPalette::ground_clutter`): the built-in Rock at three small sizes (× 0.03 to 0.06, 0.06 to 0.12 and, in loose noise patches, 0.12 to 0.25 of its 5 studs), each square to the slope (Align to slope 1) and sunk a little, visual only, 90 per 100 square studs, 0.35 studs apart. It's an ordinary palette from then on. No built-in prop is a twig or a leaf: add models of the project's own for those. Clutter is painted foliage: it counts toward the terrain's 32,768 instances (at this density about a 190-stud square holds them all), and painted built-in props have no cull distance (only plants and models do, `scene/src/foliage.rs`), so use it where the camera gets close, not across a map.
- **Decals** (the pane's Decals section; `app/decal_scatter.rs`, layout in `procedural/src/decal_scatter.rs`): a palette also holds pictures, each with **on**, **Weight**, **Size** (its longer side's smallest and largest, 0.1 to 64 studs), **Opacity** (faintest and strongest, 0 to 1) and **Rotation** (random turn on the surface; 0 lays every copy the same way, for tracks), and the picture's shape (`aspect`, read from the file when it's added, so a long track isn't squashed). **Add pictures from Assets** makes a click on an image or texture tile add it (`Picking::ScatterDecals`; one kind of picking at a time). **Paint decals** arms the same brush (same Size, `[`, `]`, Escape) to lay them instead of items: a drag makes ProjectedDecals named after their pictures in a Folder "Scattered Decals" in the Workspace, at the palette's decal **Density** (per 100 square studs, 0.05 to 50; default 2) and **Spacing** (studs between a new decal's middle and any projected decal's; default 2), one stamp every half radius, at most 64 a stamp. Each lies on the terrain under its spot, even under a vehicle or porch (the brush follows the terrain through parts), facing the ground's own direction so it isn't stretched down a slope, in a box 35% of its longer side deep (1 to 4 studs); with **Land on parts** it drops from 20 studs above the terrain onto the first terrain or colliding part (`procedural::Surfaces::below`), so it lands on porches, rocks and roofs. Shift+drag destroys the ProjectedDecals in that Folder whose middles are under the brush; decals placed by hand elsewhere are left. One stroke is one undo step ("Scatter N Decals", "Erase N Decals"); a stroke that changed nothing makes none. **Limit** (`ScatterPane::decal_budget`): the renderer draws only the nearest projected decals ([../rendering/decals.md](../rendering/decals.md)), so a stamp places no decal where 128 already lie within 150 studs (Medium's limits; **For Medium quality**, on by default) or, with it off, 1,024 within 600 (Ultra's), and says in Output how many it left out. Decals are Instances saved in `world.json`, not terrain data: they can be selected, moved and deleted like any other.
- **Brush**: **Size** (radius, 0.5 to 256 studs; `[` and `]` while armed), **Density** (items per 100 square studs, 0.1 to 200; the brush op takes per square stud, so the app divides by 100), **Spacing** (studs kept between a new item and any painted foliage, so strokes over the same ground fill gaps instead of piling up; 0 lets them touch) and **Erase everything**. The brush is full strength with a fixed soft rim (30% of the radius) and ignores the Terrain Studio's masks and symmetry.
- **Painting**: **Paint** arms the brush in the viewport through the terrain-brush plumbing (`scatter_brush_view` gives the viewport its circle, coloured like the first enabled item; while armed, `output.terrain` goes to `scatter_pointer`). Drag scatters; Shift+drag erases the palette's enabled items under the brush, or with Erase everything any painted foliage. Escape, Paint again, closing the pane, Play or picking a terrain tool puts it down; arming it puts a held terrain tool down. A stroke paints onto the Terrain Studio's terrain (the selected terrain or the first); with no terrain the pane says "Scatter paints onto Terrain", Paint is off, and Open Terrain Studio is offered. It doesn't paint onto parts. Each stroke takes the next seed (the pane's seed starts from the clock), so a stroke is deterministic for its seed and path. One stroke is one undo step, "Scatter N Items" (how many it placed) or "Erase Scatter"; a stroke that changed nothing makes none.
- **The op** (`terrain/src/brush/scatter.rs`, `Operation::Scatter(Scatter)`): per stamp, samples under the brush get `density × cell area × brush weight` tries, each jittered within its cell, skipped outside the brush, on a hole, or within the spacing of painted foliage or another new item (a hash grid of the nearby instances), then an item is picked by weight and placed with its own scale, turn, collide, lean and sink. At most 256 items a stamp and `MAX_FOLIAGE_INSTANCES` in all. Held still, a Scatter stroke doesn't stamp again.
- **Files**: palettes are the project's `presets/<name>.axscatter` (beside world presets' `.axworld`; `project::scatter_palettes`): JSON with `format` (1), `name`, `density` (per 100 square studs), `spacing` and `items`, each item's `item` written like a ScatterRule `Models` entry (`prop:Crate`, `plant:Grass` or a model path; `FoliageSource::entry` and `from_entry`) and its settings by name. An item's masks are an optional `mask` object (`noise`, `noise_scale`, `noise_threshold`, `noise_contrast`, `noise_seed`, `min_slope`, `max_slope`, `min_height`, `max_height`, `moisture`, `moisture_reach`), written only when one isn't at its default, and the palette's decals are optional `decal_density`, `decal_spacing` and `decals` (each `texture`, `enabled`, `weight`, `min_size`, `max_size`, `aspect`, `min_opacity`, `max_opacity`, `rotation`), written only when set. So the format is still 1: a palette without masks or decals is byte for byte the file it was, one written before they existed reads as it did, and an older engine reads a newer file without them (and drops them if it saves it). Every field but `item` (and a decal's `texture`) has a default; an unknown item is left out; a newer format is refused; unreadable files are reported in Output. The pane has a palette dropdown, **New** (an empty "Palette 2"…), **Rename** (moves the file; a name already taken, ignoring case, gets a number) and **Delete** (removes the file; deleting the last leaves an empty "Palette"). Edits save on their own once the mouse is let go (`ScatterPane::dirty`, saved by `scatter_frame`), and before switching palettes or projects. A project without palettes starts with an empty "Palette", written when first edited.
- **MCP**: `terrain_edit`'s `foliage` action still takes one `item`, `density` (per square stud) and `erase`, and runs it as a one-item Scatter op (labels "Paint Tree Foliage", "Erase Tree Foliage").
- **Not yet**: painting items onto parts; filling an area without a stroke (masks act under the brush only, so sweep a large brush over the ground); a preview of a mask in the viewport; moisture from water or rainfall; the decal brush on a project without Terrain; renaming or moving a model or picture (Rename or Move…, Find References) doesn't see the palettes that name it.
- **Tests**: `scatter_pane/tests.rs` (items, shares, the no-terrain card, unique names, `masks_and_decals_fit_a_narrow_column`), `app/scatter_pane_tests.rs` (the ribbon command, clicking tiles in the real Assets panel, saving and reopening, a stroke as one undo step, Shift and Erase everything, Escape, palette files; masks folded and saved and a masked stroke; the ground clutter preset; pictures clicked into decals; a decal stroke as one undo step with Shift taking them away; the limit and Land on parts), `terrain/src/brush/scatter_mask_tests.rs` (each mask's numbers, wetness on a wash and a ridge, unmasked items unmoved), `procedural/src/decal_scatter_tests.rs` (the layout, slopes, spacing, the budget), and the terrain and project tests named in [../terrain.md](../terrain.md) and `project/src/scatter_palettes.rs` (an old file reads and saves as the same bytes).
