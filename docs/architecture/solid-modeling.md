# Solid modeling

Union, Negate, Intersect and Separate (Phase 50): parts combined into one solid, a `UnionOperation`. Group as Mesh and Ungroup Mesh: parts made into one MeshPart with one optimized model, and back. The booleans are `mesh_edit` (`csg.rs`, `solid.rs`), grouping `mesh_edit` (`group.rs`, `optimize.rs`); the editor buttons `editor/src/app/solid.rs` and `app/group_mesh.rs`; the script calls `script/src/csg.rs`. Fractures, the other `mesh_edit` user, are in `fracture.md`. Why grouping makes a MeshPart with a GroupedParts inside: [ADR-0039](../../wiki/Architecture/Architecture-Decisions/ADR-0039-Group-As-Mesh-Keeps-Parts-In-A-GroupedParts.md).

## The classes

- **UnionOperation** (`core`): a BasePart drawn and collided like a MeshPart, from the model its `MeshId` names, stretched to its `Size` (`ClassName::has_model`). Its own properties: `UsePartColor` (off: each face keeps the look of the part it came from; on: all of it in `Color`) and `CollisionFidelity` (`Box`, `Hull`, the default, or `PreciseConvexDecomposition`). The parts it was made of are its children. Every Instance inside a UnionOperation is out of the world (`core::csg::in_union`, `out_of_world`): not drawn, picked, simulated or queried. The Explorer doesn't show them.
- **NegateOperation**: a Part (`is_part`, the same properties) that a union cuts out. The scene draws it pink at half its opacity (`NEGATE_COLOR`, `NEGATE_OPACITY`), in the editor and the game.
- **GroupedParts** (`core`): the parts a MeshPart made by Group as Mesh was made of, inside that MeshPart. Like a union's parts, everything inside it is out of the world (`core::csg::in_union` covers both; `in_group` is it alone; `grouped_parts(world, mesh)` finds it). `Origin` (a CFrame) and `OriginSize` are where the MeshPart was, and its Size, when it was grouped. The Explorer lists it and what's in it, dimmed (a stack icon says why); they can be selected and looked at in Properties, but the move tools never move them (`viewport::gather` leaves out what's `in_group`), so they stay where they were grouped.

## The booleans (`mesh_edit`)

- `boolean(a, b, Operation)` is the union, difference or intersection of two closed meshes, with a BSP tree of each (as csg.js does), in f64 in a unit box so one tolerance fits any size. Before the trees are built, touching triangles in one plane with one surface (and the same normals and uvs where they meet) are joined into convex polygons (`csg::join_flat`), so a quad's diagonal is never cut: kept as two triangles, a cylinder bored through a block came out with a zig-zag rim of twice the corners it needs. Splitting leaves T-junctions and hair-apart copies of a point, so corners within `SAME_POINT` are made one, each polygon gets the corners lying on its edges, and it's ear-clipped (`fill.rs`). The result is closed (`Mesh::open_edges() == 0`), so it can be combined again; `merge.rs` then refills flat faces. A flat face is matched by position, not by vertex (each boolean polygon has its own vertices), with its pieces joined where their uvs meet; it's refilled from its outline without corners inside it or in a straight line along it, then its edges are flipped until it's a Delaunay triangulation (`flip_to_delaunay`), so it has no needlessly thin triangles. Each triangle keeps its source's `Surface::Outer(i)`, so the faces of a cut keep the cutting part's look.
- `solid_of(world, assets, part)` makes a `Solid`: a Part's (or NegateOperation's) shape at its `Size` and its look (`generate::part_look`), or a MeshPart's or UnionOperation's model stretched to its `Size` with its materials. A Capsule, a model that can't load, or a mesh with open edges is refused with a message that names the part.
- `combine(keep, cut, operation)` works in the first kept part's frame: the kept solids are unioned (or intersected), then each cut one subtracted. The result is centered: its `position`, the first part's `rotation`, and `size` its box. Parts with identical looks share one surface, and at the end faces with a plain look (no texture) are merged once more even where their pieces' uvs don't meet, their uvs spread from 0 to 1 across the merged face as on a Part (`merge_flat_faces` with `plain`): two blocks side by side union into a box of 8 corners and 12 triangles. Looks no triangle uses are dropped. Nothing left is an error. `combine_parts` sorts the parts: NegateOperations are cut.
- `Combined::glb()` writes one object at its size in studs; `save_union` writes `assets/models/<name>.union.glb` (numbered if taken).

## Grouping (`mesh_edit`)

- `group_source(world, assets, part)` makes a `GroupSource` of a Part (its shape at its `Size`), MeshPart or UnionOperation (its model stretched to its `Size`): its triangles, its looks (`GroupLook`: a `ModelMaterial`'s values and the texture files it links by project path, `MaterialMaps`), where it is, whether it's drawn (`Transparency` below 1) and the convex pieces it collides as. Nothing has to be closed. A Part's look is its color (linear), Metalness, Roughness and its Material's opacity and glow; a model's are its materials, or a SurfaceAppearance's maps where one names the material (as `scene` draws it: tint, color, normal, metalness and roughness when glTF's one blue-green texture can hold them, red occlusion, glow, Tiling and Offset); a UnionOperation with `UsePartColor` is its Color. `Transparency` is baked into each look's alpha. What can't be carried is a note naming the part (Output warns): a built-in Material's texture (Wood, Brick and the rest are grouped in their color), a MaterialVariant, a Part's SurfaceAppearance, an opacity or specular map, a DirectX normal map, Decals. A Capsule is refused.
- Collision pieces: CanCollide off gives none; a Part its corners (one convex piece); a model by its `CollisionFidelity`: Box its box, Hull its hull, Default its own `_COL` pieces or else its hull. The editor gives a UnionOperation with PreciseConvexDecomposition its decomposed pieces (`physics::precise_pieces`).
- `group(sources)` puts them in the first one's frame (a part turned the same way isn't turned, so its corners land exactly), gives parts that look the same one material, runs `optimize`, drops unused looks and centers the result: `Grouped` (mesh, looks, collision pieces, position, rotation, size, and `GroupStats` with `summary()`). Nothing to draw is an error.
- `optimize(mesh, part_of, parts, looks)` (`optimize.rs`) never changes what's seen. A part is *solid* (`PartInfo`) when it's closed (`open_edges() == 0`), has volume and every look is opaque; only solid parts hide anything, so open meshes (planes, leaves) are grouped as they are. In order: **weld** (corners within a millionth of the group's size become one point; vertices at a point with the same normal and uv one vertex, so hard edges and uv seams stay); **degenerate** (no area) and **duplicate** (same corners, same facing, same look) triangles go; **pressed together**: a triangle with an exact opposite twin in another solid part goes, and so does one that opposite faces of other solid parts in its plane cover completely between them (cut out of it in 2D, plane by plane, through a grid); one only partly covered stays whole (cutting it would add triangles); **hidden inside**: a triangle whose seen side (both, for a two-sided look) is inside another solid part goes: moved a hair (4 millionths of the group's size) forward and in from its edges, it must cross none of that part's triangles (a box hierarchy finds them) and its middle and corners must be inside (a ray along +X counts crossings with exact orientation tests and a top-left rule, so a ray through an edge counts once); **coplanar merge** (`merge_flat_faces_with` with `same_uv_map`): touching flat triangles with one look and normal are filled again from their outline without inner or in-line corners, Delaunay-flipped, a corner another face needs kept on both sides (no T-junction cracks); a textured face only joins where its uvs are one flat mapping across the pieces; plain faces first split their outline edges at other plain faces' corners lying on them (T-junctions become shared edges), kept only when that ends with fewer triangles; then unused vertices go. Running it again changes nothing.
- `Grouped::glb(folder)` writes one object `Group`, a primitive per look (`File::add_material_with`: values, textures linked relative to `folder`, `KHR_texture_transform`, wind `extras`), and each collision piece as `Group_COL1`, `Group_COL2`, … (not drawn; the MeshPart's Default fidelity collides as them). `save_group` writes `<folder>/<name>.group.glb` (numbered if taken), where `folder` is `assets/models` or, when a look links textures elsewhere, the folder holding them all (a model's textures must be inside its own folder; `assets` for SurfaceAppearance textures in `assets/textures`).
- Measured (debug test build): 290 parts and 101,740 triangles optimize in 0.2 s, 5,000 parts and 60,000 triangles meeting on one plane in 0.44 s (`optimize_tests`, ignored measurements), so it runs on the UI thread.

## The editor (Model ▸ Solid Modeling)

`SolidAction`: each is one `History::record_world` step, disabled during Play. The parts are the selected parts and the parts in selected Models (`solid_parts`, not ones already inside a union).

- **Union** / **Intersect** (two or more parts): `combine_parts`, then `save_union` in the project. The UnionOperation, named `Union` (numbered among its siblings), takes the first part's place, with the first part's `Anchored`, `Color`, `CastShadow`, `Transparency`, collision and physical properties; the parts are moved inside it, and it's selected. A failure goes to Output ("Could not union the parts: … Nothing was changed.") with no step and no file. Undo keeps the `.glb`, like a Fracture's pieces.
- **Negate**: each selected Part becomes a NegateOperation and each NegateOperation a Part (`replace_class`: name, properties both have, attributes, tags and children; new IDs, selected).
- **Separate**: each selected UnionOperation's children go back to its parent, right after it, and it leaves the tree. The move tools move a union's children with it (they're its descendants), so separated parts are where the union was moved to.
- **Group as Mesh** (`app/group_mesh.rs`; two or more parts): the selection's roots (selected parts, Models and Folders, not locked or out of the world) and the parts in them (NegateOperations left out) become one MeshPart: `group_source` for each, `group`, `save_group`. The MeshPart, named after the Model when one Model is grouped and `Group` (numbered) otherwise, takes the first root's place with `MeshId`, `Position`, `Orientation`, `Size`, and the first part's Anchored, CastShadow, CanCollide, CanTouch, CanQuery, CollisionGroup, CustomPhysicalProperties and Massless; a GroupedParts inside it records where it is and holds the roots, structure and all. One step ("Group N parts as a mesh"); Output says "Grouped 12 parts: 4,812 → 1,206 triangles, … vertices (triangles taken away: …). Saved in …" and warns for each note. A failure says why and changes nothing. The Explorer's right-click menu has it for parts, Models and Folders. MeshParts collide by Default fidelity, which is the `_COL` pieces: the group collides as its parts did, concave or not, so it doesn't need PreciseConvexDecomposition.
- **Ungroup Mesh** (a MeshPart with a GroupedParts): its kept parts move, turn and stretch as the MeshPart has since grouping (`Origin`, `OriginSize`: each position in the old frame, scaled by the size over the size then, in the new frame; each Size along its own axes by that stretch; free-standing lights and emitters move too), go back to its parent right after it, in order, and it leaves the tree. Not moved, they come back exactly as they were. One step ("Ungroup mesh into N parts"); also on the Explorer's right-click menu.
- **To Terrain** (`app/to_terrain.rs`; needs a Terrain in the Workspace): each selected part becomes ground on the first Terrain. The heightfield has one height per sample, so every sample under the part rises (never lowers) to the part's top there, found by a ray straight down onto it (`scene::part_hit`: a ball makes a dome, a wedge a ramp), clamped to the Terrain's height range (Output warns when cut off), and is painted fully with the TerrainLayer named after the part's Material, or else nearest its Color (`terrain::convert::raise_to`). The converted parts are deleted and the Terrain is selected, all one step ("Convert N parts to terrain"). Parts off the terrain are left, and with none on it Output says so and nothing changes.
- **Conform to Terrain** and **Blend into Terrain** (procedural worlds PW5, `app/terrain_objects.rs`; need a Terrain): each selected part or model (one inside another selected one counts with it) is a group of what Move would move and its parts' boxes (`procedural::obstacles::Obstacle::part_box`). Conform moves each group straight up or down so its lowest corner sits on the ground under the middle of its footprint (the first Terrain there): one step, "Conform to Terrain". Blend sets every terrain sample under the group's parts seen from above to its bottom and paints it with the Terrain Studio's Paint layer, fading both out over 6 studs around (`procedural::blend_footprint`): one step, "Blend into Terrain". Tests: `editor/src/app/terrain_object_tests.rs`, `procedural/src/objects_tests.rs`.

## Collision fidelity (`physics`)

`Form::Mesh(model, Fidelity)`: `Box` is the part's box; `Hull` the convex hull MeshParts have; `Precise` is parry's `convex_decomposition_with_params` over the model's welded triangles with full-resolution planes and exact hulls (`shapes::PRECISE`; with parry's defaults a doorway's corner got a slanted piece no character could pass). Decomposing takes a while (half a second for the example doorway, a few seconds for a crater), so pieces are kept by model key and size (`decomposed`, up to 256), and colliders built again for queries and masses reuse them. MeshParts always use `Hull`.

## Scripts

`part:UnionAsync(parts, options?)`, `SubtractAsync` and `IntersectAsync` exist on every BasePart class. The call reads the solids from the world (errors raise at once), then runs `combine` on a thread of its own, which also reads the `.glb` back and, for `options.CollisionFidelity = PreciseConvexDecomposition`, makes the convex pieces (`physics::prepare_precise`), and pauses the script (a Lua wrapper yields, as `InvokeServer` does). `csg::step`, in `Context::step` after the scheduler, hands back finished jobs: the model goes into the `Assets` as `:runtime/union<N>.glb` (never a file, so a save slot that keeps a runtime union reloads it as a grey box), and a new UnionOperation with Parent nil and the calling part's look and physics resumes the script; a failure raises its reason there. Union and Intersect cut NegateOperations among `parts`; Subtract cuts all of them. A stopped script's jobs are forgotten.
