# Screen-space reflections

Smooth and wet surfaces reflect what's on the screen (trees, houses, the bright haze down a street), and the sky's reflection is shaded where something stands over the way it leaves (a canopy, an eave). Without them a solid surface reflects only the reflection probes and the sky's image, which nothing stands in front of: under a forest's canopy every wet hollow mirrored the bright overcast sky as a white blotch (Overgrown Street, 2026-11-26). Added 2026-11-26.

## Setting

`GraphicsSettings::screen_space_reflections` (`graphics.json`'s `screen_space_reflections`, Settings > Graphics > **Screen-space reflections**): on at High and Ultra, off at Low and Medium. It needs compute shaders; without them it's off with a note ("Screen-space reflections are off: …"). The editor's In use list and the benchmark's changes table list it. Ray traced reflections ([ray-tracing.md](ray-tracing.md#ray-traced-reflections-rl4)), when drawn, take its place: `screen_reflections::marched` is the setting with ray traced reflections not drawn.

## The pass

`renderer/screen_reflections.rs` and `screen_reflections.wgsl` (`FramePass::ScreenReflections`, "Screen-space reflections" in the profiler, after ray traced reflections and before volumetric fog): a compute pass over every pixel of the scene (8 × 8 groups) after the prepass.

- **Which pixels.** A plain solid pixel of the prepass smoother than 0.45 (`ROUGHNESS_CUTOFF`; the prepass normal's w is 1 plus the roughness, `fs_prepass`, and terrain's `fs_terrain_prepass`). Sky, characters' and cut-outs' pixels (leaves, grass) aren't marched. The prepass reads no maps, so its roughness is the least the scene pass can find there: wet ground is marked as if water had gathered everywhere it could (`weathered_prepass`), and the scene pass fades by the roughness it finds.
- **The march.** One ray along the mirrored view (the prepass's normal, without the normal map), from just off the surface, out to 400 studs or just short of the camera's plane. It is marched across the screen in pixels, interpolating 1 over the clip w so the distance along it stays right (perspective): 40 steps, the first 0.75 to 2.25 pixels, each 1.14 times the last (about 2,700 pixels in all), until it leaves the screen. Where it passes behind what the prepass's depth shows (a depth `d` is `w / (d + z)` studs ahead, from the projection's `z_axis.z` and `w_axis.z`), five halvings narrow it down to the crossing. It hit that thing if it's no more than its thickness behind it (0.3 studs, or 3.5% of its distance if more, `SSR_THICKNESS`); further behind, it went behind it, and the march goes on. The first step's length moves with TAA's frame (interleaved gradient noise), so TAA averages the steps; without TAA it holds still.
- **What it sees.** Last frame's picture: the scene's color target (`FrameTargets::hdr`, linear light before bloom and tone mapping, after TAA or motion blur when they're on), which still holds last frame until this frame's scene pass draws over it. The hit's world point is found from this frame's depth and put on last frame's screen with last frame's camera (without TAA's jitter: TAA's result has none). A forward renderer lights each pixel in one pass, so there's no picture of this frame to read before its surfaces are lit; the same choice as HDRP's screen-space reflections, which read last frame's color pyramid. A rougher surface's reflection is read over its lobe's footprint: four more reads at roughness² × half the ray's length on the screen (at most 24 pixels), turned by the noise.
- **How much it counts.** Its weight fades out within 12% of the screen's edges (at the hit, and where it was last frame), for a hit on the far side of something (its normal facing along the ray, from 0.1), for rays coming back toward the camera (from −0.1 to −0.5 along its forward) and over the last fifth of the steps. A miss, a ray that leaves the screen, a hit off last frame's screen or a pixel not marched writes 0.
- **Written** as half floats, two `u32`s a pixel, in the buffer ray traced reflections use, bound in the frame's place 18 (`bind_frame`: the ray traced buffer if drawn, else this one, else a one-entry stand-in). `Frame::ray_reflections` is (0.45, 1, the width, 1): its w says the reflections are on the screen.
- **First frames.** A frame whose last frame wasn't drawn into the same targets (the first one, or after the targets are made again) clears the buffer (it was made with `COPY_DST` for this) in place of marching: nothing is found on the screen, while the sky's shading below is already on. Each following frame reads the one before, so a change settles over a frame or two, and reflections of reflections appear a frame later each. Tests that compare a first frame with later ones draw without them.

## In the scene

`lighting.wgsl`'s `traced_reflected`, in `shade_surface`'s reflection, for solid surfaces:

- **What's on the screen** takes the probes' and the sky's place by its weight times `1 − smoothstep(0.55 × 0.45, 0.45, roughness)`: whole up to about 0.25 (the wet range), gone by the cutoff. Ray traced reflections fade by `(1 − roughness / cutoff)²` instead, into the probes' blur; on the screen there's no blur to fade into but the sky's.
- **Specular occlusion.** The reflection was and is multiplied by the surface's occlusion (its map's and screen-space ambient occlusion's). Now the sky's share of it is also shaded by **sky visibility along the reflection** (`sky_open_along`): the sun's shadow maps are asked at four points along it (1.5, 4, 9 and 20 studs, times 0.75 to 1.25 by the shading noise; `SKY_OPEN_STEPS`) whether something stands close over them on the way toward the sun (`sky_over`: one read of the nearest cascade covering the point; what stands within 10 studs hides the sky, fading out to nothing by 35, `SKY_BLOCKER`, so a canopy or an eave counts and a tree whose long shadow falls on open ground doesn't; a point no cascade reaches counts as open), the nearest counting most (0.35, 0.3, 0.2, 0.15: `SKY_OPEN_WEIGHTS`). (A first version counted every point in the sun's shadow: open lawns in a tree's shadow lost their sky's sheen.) A way that stays under something loses up to 0.9 of the sky (`SKY_OPEN_SHADE`), which is replaced by what a dark underside lit by the whole sky gives back (`SKY_BLOCKED_ALBEDO` 0.12 of the ambient and the sky's light from above), so a puddle under a tree is dark, not black. A way heading down (it sees the ground's part of the sky) isn't shaded, nor is anything without the sun's shadows, and it fades out with the shadows toward their reach. Reflection probes keep their own bake. Only while screen-space reflections are drawn; ray traced reflections see what's over the way themselves.
- Where water fills a surface's cracks, their occlusion no longer darkens the water's top (`filled_occlusion`: 0.6 of it is lifted, by how much of the surface the water has levelled; [weather.md](weather.md)).

See-through surfaces, water and leaves keep the probes and the sky (water has its own screen-space reflection, [water.md](water.md)).

## Cost

One buffer of 8 bytes a pixel (28.8 MB at 2560 × 1405) and a compute pass; the scene pass reads one more storage entry a pixel and, for solid pixels, four shadow-map reads for the sky's shading. Measured 2026-11-26 on the Desktop of the wiki's Hardware notes (RX 9070 XT, radv; release player, `--bench 600`, Ultra, 2560 × 1405, a copy of Overgrown Street at Wetness 0.5, two runs each, noisy: other agents shared the machine): the pass 0.077–0.078 ms, the scene pass 9.41 → 10.32–10.38 ms, as shown 43.4 → 41.4–41.5 frames a second. Nearly all of the millisecond is the sky's shading in the scene pass ([Optimization log](../../wiki/Performance/Optimization-Log.md)).

## Tests

- `render/src/renderer/tests/screen_reflections.rs` (GPU): a smooth black floor shows a glowing red block standing on it from the second frame, and nothing on the first; the floor beside the reflection stays dark (`a_smooth_floor_mirrors_a_block_on_the_screen_from_the_second_frame`); a floor rougher than the cutoff with no sun draws exactly as with them off (`a_rough_floor_draws_as_with_them_off`); turning them off frees the pass and its bytes and draws as before (`turning_them_off_frees_the_pass_and_draws_as_before`); a floor under a roof above the frame doesn't mirror the bright sky the roof hides (less than half as bright as with them off) while the open floor beside it still does (`a_floor_under_a_roof_does_not_mirror_the_sky_the_roof_hides`).
- `render/src/renderer/screen_reflections.rs`: a depth comes back as its distance from the camera.
- `render-types/src/graphics.rs`: on at High and Ultra, the override, off with a note without compute shaders, saved only when set.
- `editor-benchmark/src/tests.rs`: the changes table has its row.
