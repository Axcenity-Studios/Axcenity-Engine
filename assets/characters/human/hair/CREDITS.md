# Hair credits

Every hairstyle here is a MakeHuman community asset, converted for Axcenity's human base by
`assets/characters/human/hair.py` with the MPFB add-on (https://static.makehumancommunity.org/mpfb.html).
The meshes were re-posed, re-skinned to the Axcenity Humanoid skeleton and given the body's shapes;
textures are as their authors made them. Source: https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks.html

All are CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): credit isn't asked for, and is
given all the same.
