# `anim`

- One clip type (`files::ClipFile`, `*.anim.json`: tracks, events, an optional take `link`), state machines (`*.statemachine.json`, with layers and 2D blend spaces since Phase 78: `blend_space.rs`, `CompiledLayer`; tests `layer_tests.rs`), `Clip`, `Compiled`/`Machine` and `Animators`. Bone paths, `Bone` key numbers and Convert to keys: `bones.rs`; cleanup of keyed clips (mirror, smooth, reduce keys, loop seam, re-time; AP4): `cleanup.rs`. Humanoid roles (body and fingers; rig definitions from `assets/rigs/*.rig.json` through `LoadedClip`), checking skeletons, retarget profiles (`*.retarget.json` named by a link) and retargeting a take onto another skeleton (G16, AP1, AP2): `retarget.rs` and `retarget/`. Faces (F3): `face.rs` turns a FaceControls' standard controls into blend shape weights and eye and jaw turns through a face profile (built-ins in `faces/`), applied last in `Animators`; F4's layers (expressions, overrides, blinks, gaze, idle) are `face_play.rs` (pure, tests there) and `face_world.rs` (what's read from the World), built-in expressions in `faces/expressions/`; F5's lip sync (viseme blending, the built-in map in `faces/visemes/`, generation from phonemes and recordings) is `lip_sync.rs`; `pose_face` and `face_point` pose and frame Character Studio's preview. Inverse kinematics (posing joints, pins; plain math on a `Rig` and `Pose`): `ik.rs`; at runtime (Phase 79): `IKControl`s in `ik_control.rs`, `FootPlacement` in `feet.rs`, which needs a `GroundProbe` from the host (`Animators::with_ground`, `character::foot_ground`). Key easings and Bezier handles: `easing.rs` (shared with tweens; every track samples through `sample_track`). Open one of: `docs/rendering/animation.md` (playback), `docs/scripting/api-animation.md` (Lua), `docs/project/animation-files.md` (files).
- Depends only on `core` and `assets`. Read files through `Assets`; skeletal poses from `Model::pose`.
- `Animators` is the only host: one runner per animated thing plays its Animator's state machine, then AnimationTracks and played Animations over it (latest start wins). A Ragdoll in a MeshPart gets a runner too (Phase 41, `ragdoll.rs`: Build, the humanoid preset, `pose_from_bodies`); its pose overrides the Animator's while it's on and blends back over `BlendTime`. `Animators::inspect` (`animators/inspect.rs`, AP6) is a read-only snapshot of one runner for the editor's Animator Inspector.
- Live state is `core::AnimatorState`, `AnimationTrackState` and `PropertyAnimationState` in the `World`, not saved properties.
- Followers (HD4): skinned MeshParts inside a posed one with no runner of their own follow its pose by bone name and are placed by it (`follow`, `place_follower`; HD4), and each of their blend shapes takes the leader's live weight of the shape with the same name (`same_shapes`; shapes the leader lacks keep their own weight; a part's own joint-driven shapes, a garment's pose correctives, ADR-0044, are then driven from its pose: `correctives::drive_own`), and an Animator with nothing to play still poses its MeshPart at rest when it has MeshPart children (`leads` in `update_one`), so a still character's parts are placed; body sliders (`body_shape.rs`, attributes on the MeshPart) and sockets (`sockets.rs`, Attachments with a `Bone` attribute, and accessories on them) run there too. A character's identity bone changes are added right after the clips (`Model::settle` for a baked model, `LiveIdentity::settle` for one worn live: R2a, ADR-0041, the `Identity` attribute and the per-model cache in `live_identity.rs`), before body sliders, the ragdoll, IK and the feet, so every clip plays on every character; keep that order in both posing paths. Its shape weights and joint corrections go on last (`LiveIdentity::dress` in `skinned`, and on followers after the leader's shapes). Twist and helper bones (CO4, `twist.rs`: found by name, rules in its module docs; a skeleton without them is untouched) are turned last, at one call site (`skinned` in `animators.rs`), just before the skin is posed, so followers get them too; the realistic body's QA scripts copy the rule, so change both. A take named like the model that keys bone offsets on a skeleton of other proportions in the same rest pose plays through roles, not by name (`reshapes` in `clip.rs`), so base models that share bone names keep their own bone lengths. `ragdoll::refit` keeps a MeshPart where it stands when its model changes to one with another fit.
- `sequence.rs` (Phase 51): cutscene sequences (`*.sequence.json`, bindings by path or `tag:`) and `SequenceRun::apply`, which the editor's preview and the Sequence Instance share; `docs/project/sequences.md`.
- Format 1 files still read (`ClipFile::from_json` converts them). Keep example files canonical (`tests.rs` checks `to_json` round-trips them).
